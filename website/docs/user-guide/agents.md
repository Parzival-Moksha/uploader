# Agents

Every agent in the village is a real Claude Code session (or a Hermes agent) on your machine. What you say to it in the village arrives in that session; what it does, it does in its folder, under your own plan.

## Summon

- **Agents → Summon an agent**, the spell book's **Agents → Summon Claude Code** (it then appears in front of you), or **E** in the circle on the plaza.
- **New session:** a name, the model, the permissions (from "ask before acting" to "full access"), an avatar, an optional "who it is" line for its card, and the folder it works in (shown quietly; **change** picks another).
- **Continue session:** your existing sessions, newest first. One that is open in another window can be summoned as a copy.
- **Hermes:** pick one of the Hermes profiles on this machine. Codex and phone-call agents are shown but not ready yet.

## Talk

- Walk up to an agent: the keys show above it. **E** its panel, **F** its full screen, **T** talk by voice. **N** jumps to the next agent that needs you.
- An agent that needs a decision shows an exclamation mark and a card in its panel: allow, deny, or answer. Nothing is approved for you.
- Each reply ends with one spoken line. It is said in your browser's own voice, or with ElevenLabs if you add a key.

## Look after them

- **Settings** in an agent's panel: its name, who it is, avatar, voice, when it wakes on its own, where it lives.
- **Dismiss** (or **Delete** with the agent selected) ends its process; the session itself stays in Claude Code and can be continued later.
- If every Claude account on the machine is out of usage, the summon menu says so and until when; an agent summoned then waits and goes on by itself.
