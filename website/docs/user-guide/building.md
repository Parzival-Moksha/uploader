# Building

Two ways to change a world: ask an agent, or cast it yourself.

## Ask an agent

Any agent in the village can see and change the world it stands in: place catalog objects, build small scenes from simple shapes, set the sky, the ground and the lights, and take a picture of what you see. Say what you want in plain words. Those tools are free and need no key.

## The spell book (B)

- **Create:** objects from the catalog (trees, rocks, buildings, furniture); pick one, then click where it goes.
- **World:** the background (sky), the ground's texture, its elevation, lights, and portals to other worlds.
- **Creative:** the brush wand (paint lines and marks into the world) and 3D text.
- **Own:** your own pictures, video and sound, placed in the world.
- **Premium:** pictures, video, music and 3D models made from a sentence. Each needs your own key for that service and costs money there; the spell says which key.
- **Combat:** bolts, for play mode.
- **Agents:** summon an agent (see [Agents](./agents.md)).

The **Wizard** button in the spell book opens the console: everything placed in this world, the catalog, and media.

## Move what is placed

Click an object to select it: the object panel shows how to move, turn and scale it, and how to delete it. **Esc** lets go.

## Worlds

**World** (top left) lists your worlds: open one, make a new one, or go back. Everything you build is saved with the world.
