# Your first minutes

1. **Look around.** You stand on the plaza of the village. Walk with W A S D; click the world, then the mouse turns the view.
2. **Meet your sessions.** On the first run, your newest Claude Code sessions from this computer stand in front of you, each named after its folder. Walk up to one and press **E**: you read what it last did. Nothing runs until you ask.
3. **Carry one on.** **Talk to it here** in its panel continues that same session in the village, on your own plan. Type and press Enter, or press **T** and talk. **F** shows its full screen.
4. **Summon a guide.** **Agents** (top left) → **Summon a guide**: a Claude Code session named Guide, in a new folder `Oasis` in your home, says hello out loud and tells you the keys. With no sessions on this computer, start here.
5. **Build.** Ask an agent to build something ("put three palm trees by the pool"), or open the spell book (**B**) and place things yourself.

The [keys](../user-guide/keys.md) are on one page. If something looks wrong, see [When something is wrong](../reference/troubleshooting.md).
